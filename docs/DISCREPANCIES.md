| Item | Printed | Reality |
|---|---|---|
| Soft Drink | 67 | plausible only for a small diet drink |
| Kenza | 62 | ? |
| **Water** | **75** | **0 — water has no calories** |

as NULL rather than publish known-bad figures. Needs real values.
